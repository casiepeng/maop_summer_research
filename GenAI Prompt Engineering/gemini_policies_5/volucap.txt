Based on the provided privacy policy from volucap.com/privacy-policy-voluverse/, here is a summary without adding outside information or omitting key details:

Volucap GmbH acts as the data controller for the processing of your personal data.

**Data Collection and Processing:**

1.  **When Visiting the Website:**
    *   **What:** The website collects data in server log files, including your IP address, browser type, operating system, referrer URL, and time of access. Essential cookies are also used for website functionality.
    *   **Why:** This data is processed for system security, operation, error analysis, and to ensure network and information security.
    *   **Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
    *   **Retention:** Server log files are stored for a maximum of 7 days.

2.  **When Contacting Volucap (via Email or Contact Form):**
    *   **What:** If you contact them, they collect your email address, name, and any other information you provide in your message.
    *   **Why:** To process your request and communicate with you.
    *   **Legal Basis:** Legitimate interest (to respond to inquiries), for the initiation or fulfillment of a contract, or based on your consent.
    *   **Retention:** Data is stored until your request is fully processed or as long as required by legal retention periods.

3.  **Regarding the Voluverse App:**
    *   **Crucially, the Voluverse App DOES NOT store or transmit any personal data to Volucap GmbH.**
    *   **No Internet Connection Required:** The app functions completely offline.
    *   **Local Storage Only:** All app data, such as game progress or settings, is stored *only locally* on your device. Volucap does not have access to this data.
    *   **No Tracking or Analytics:** The Voluverse App does not collect or send any analytics data, user behavior, or other information to Volucap.

**Data Recipients:**

*   Your personal data is generally not shared with third parties, except where legally required or when necessary for the technical operation of the website (e.g., with a web hoster or IT service provider, who act as processors under strict agreements).
*   **No data from the Voluverse App is shared** as it does not transmit any data.

**Data Transfer Outside EU/EEA:**
*   There are no plans for data transfer to countries outside the EU/EEA. If such a transfer were to occur, appropriate safeguards (like standard contractual clauses) would be implemented.

**Your Rights (under GDPR):**
You have the right to:
*   **Information/Access** (Art. 15): Request details about your processed data.
*   **Rectification** (Art. 16): Correct inaccurate personal data.
*   **Erasure** (Art. 17): Request deletion of your data.
*   **Restriction of processing** (Art. 18): Limit how your data is used.
*   **Data Portability** (Art. 20): Receive your data in a structured, common format.
*   **Objection** (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
*   **Withdraw Consent** (Art. 7(3)): Revoke any given consent at any time.
*   **Lodge a complaint** (Art. 77): With a supervisory authority.

You can contact Volucap GmbH's Data Protection Officer for any questions regarding data protection.
