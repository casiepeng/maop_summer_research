AuraVision's Privacy Policy details that its products rely heavily on **sensors** to collect various types of data crucial for their functionality. Here's a summary of the key sensor-related data points:

1.  **Biometric Information:** This is highly personal data collected by the sensors to understand and track the user's physical presence and interactions. It includes:
    *   **Eye-tracking and Gaze Estimation:** What the user is looking at.
    *   **Head Pose and Facial Features:** The orientation of the user's head and characteristics of their face.
    *   **Body Movements:** Detailed information such as skeletal data, depth data, and point cloud data, indicating the ability to track full body movements and create a 3D understanding of the user's form.

2.  **Environmental Information:** Sensors also capture data about the user's physical surroundings, including:
    *   **Spatial Mapping Data:** Details about room dimensions, the layout of the environment, and the placement of objects within it.
    *   **Ambient Light Levels:** The lighting conditions in the user's surroundings.
    *   **Acoustic Information:** Sounds from the user's environment, implying the use of microphones.

**Purpose of Sensor Data Collection:**
This extensive sensor data is essential for the core operation of AuraVision's products. It is used to:
*   Enable **intuitive control and interaction** with their devices (e.g., using gaze or body movements for input).
*   **Enhance the overall user experience**.
*   Develop **new features** related to "spatial computing" and "human-computer interaction."
*   Support **research and development** efforts, often utilizing anonymized or aggregated forms of this data.

**Data Sharing:**
While not exclusively for sensor data, the policy notes that your information (which includes this sensor-derived data) may be shared with third-party service providers for services like data storage and analytics, or disclosed if legally required or during business transfers.

In essence, the sensors are fundamental to how AuraVision's products work, gathering highly detailed information about both the user's body and their surrounding environment to facilitate an immersive and interactive experience.
