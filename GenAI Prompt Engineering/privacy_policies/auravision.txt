Photon
To enable real-time multiplayer, matchmaking, and voice chat, Photon generates and stores a unique token associated with you, which remains in your possession. Photon also temporarily stores your IP address during a match and retains this data for up to one month to facilitate real-time networking. However, the data stored by Photon is pseudonymized and cannot be used to personally identify you.

For more details, you can review Photon's privacy policy here.

User & Parents Rights
Parents have ongoing rights regarding their child's personal information:

Review: Parents can access and review their child's personal data.
Revocation of Consent: Parents may withdraw their consent and prevent further collection or usage of their child's information.
Deletion: Parents can request the deletion of their child's personal data. We take appropriate steps to verify that we are communicating with the child's parents when handling these requests.
Meta Quest User Age Group APIS
We utilize Meta's Get Age Category API to determine the user's age group. For users under 13, we impose specific restrictions to avoid collecting personal data and implement gameplay changes to ensure privacy and safety. Users younger than 10 are not permitted to access the game. Since we do not require age data for gameplay, we do not utilize the Report Age Category API from Meta.

Child Personal Information Protection
At Aura Vision, we are dedicated to safeguarding the confidentiality, security, and integrity of children's personal information. We limit data collection to what is necessary, retain it only for as long as needed, and securely dispose of it once it is no longer required.
