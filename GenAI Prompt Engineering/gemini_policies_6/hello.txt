Based on the VRChat Privacy Policy (last updated August 23, 2023), the following information is collected via sensors during the application's runtime:

1.  **Audio Data from Microphone:**
    The policy states that audio data is collected through your device's microphone to facilitate voice chat features.
    "When you use VRChat, other users may be able to hear your voice, and we may collect audio data through your device’s microphone to enable voice chat features."

2.  **Movement, Gaze, and Other Interactions:**
    Information related to user inputs, including physical movement and gaze, is collected while using the services. This type of data is typically derived from device sensors.
    "This includes information regarding user inputs (e.g., button presses, movement, gaze, and other interactions within the Services)."

3.  **Video Information from Camera:**
    If enabled by the user, the device's camera may be used to collect video information for specific in-service features.
    "If you enable your camera and/or video settings within the Services, we may collect video information (including still images) through your device’s camera to facilitate specific in-Service features (e.g., avatar capture)."
