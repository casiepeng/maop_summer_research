The Voluverse privacy policy outlines the collection of information from various sensors while the application is running, primarily to enhance the user's experience within the virtual environment.

Here's a summary of the policy regarding sensor data collection:

*   **Movement and Orientation Data**: Voluverse collects data from your device's gyroscope, accelerometer, and potentially other sensors. This is done "while you are using Voluverse to determine your position and movements within the virtual environment."
*   **Other Device Sensors (Depth Sensors, Cameras for Specific Features)**: The application "may also collect data from other device sensors, such as depth sensors or cameras, if your device has them and they are used by Voluverse for specific features (e.g., environmental mapping, hand tracking, or avatar pose estimation)." This data "is typically processed locally on your device and used to enhance your experience within the application."
*   **Camera and Microphone Data (General Use)**: Voluverse clarifies that it "does not typically record or store raw camera or microphone data." If a specific feature requires access, such as "for avatar customization based on a photo, or for voice chat in a social experience," users "will be prompted for your explicit consent." Any such data "is primarily processed in real-time for the intended feature and not stored long-term by Volucap, unless explicitly stated and agreed upon for a specific service."
*   **Location Data**: While Voluverse "does not directly collect precise GPS location data," your "IP address might indicate your approximate geographical region." Furthermore, "if you enable location services for your device and for Voluverse (e.g., for features that depend on knowing your general area), the application might access this data." However, the policy states, "this is not a core function for most Voluverse experiences."
