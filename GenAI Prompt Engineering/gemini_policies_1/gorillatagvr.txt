### 👓 Gorilla Tag VR: Your Sensors & Your Privacy

The Gorilla Tag VR privacy policy outlines that **certain sensor-derived data can be collected, but only if you actively choose to enable specific features within the application.** Think of it like optional upgrades that use your VR headset's advanced capabilities.

---

#### **1. Where You Are (Precise Geolocation Data)**

*   **What it is:** This data tells the application your very precise physical location.
*   **How it's captured:** It uses technologies like your device's GPS or Wi-Fi signals.
*   **Visual & Explanation:**
    *   `📍 GPS Location`
    *   `📡 Wi-Fi Positioning`
    *   **In Simple Terms:** Imagine your VR headset knowing exactly where you are in the real world, down to a few feet, thanks to signals it picks up.

---

#### **2. How You Move & Express (Biometric Data)**

*   **What it is:** This refers to data about your unique physical movements and expressions.
*   **How it's captured:** Through the headset's internal tracking systems.
*   **Visual & Explanation:**
    *   `✋ Hand Tracking` (e.g., the position and movement of your fingers and hands)
    *   `👁️ Eye Tracking` (e.g., where your gaze is directed)
    *   `😊 Facial Expressions` (e.g., your smiles, frowns, blinks as detected by the headset)
    *   **In Simple Terms:** This is the game understanding how you're using your hands, where you're looking, and even the expressions on your face, if your headset supports it and you turn it on.

---

#### **3. Your Physical State (Health Data)**

*   **What it is:** Data related to your body's physical metrics during gameplay.
*   **How it's captured:** If integrated with specific health-tracking features or devices.
*   **Visual & Explanation:**
    *   `❤️ Heart Rate` (e.g., how fast your heart is beating)
    *   `🔥 Calories Burned` (e.g., an estimate of your energy expenditure)
    *   **In Simple Terms:** If you connect a fitness tracker or your VR system measures these, the game *could* know how much you're exerting yourself.

---

#### **The MOST Important Point: Your Choice!**

The policy explicitly states:

*   **"if you choose to enable such features or provide us with such information."**
*   **Visual & Explanation:**
    *   `✅ Opt-In Required`
    *   `🎮 User Control`
    *   **In Simple Terms:** None of this precise sensor data (geolocation, advanced biometrics, or health data) is collected without your active permission. You have to *choose* to turn on features that use this information. It's not collected by default simply by playing the game.

---

#### **Why is this data collected (if you opt-in)?**

While the policy doesn't list a specific purpose for *each* sensor type in isolation, it generally states that collected information (including this sensor data if you opt-in) is used to:
*   **Improve and personalize your experience.**
*   **Develop new features and functionality** within the game or application.
*   **Understand how you use the game.**

Essentially, if you enable these sensor-based features, the data helps make your VR experience more immersive, responsive, or provides new gameplay elements based on your real-world movements and status.
