### Immersed Privacy Policy: Understanding Sensor Data

Immersed, being a VR application, interacts with your VR headset's built-in sensors to provide its core functionality. The policy outlines which sensor data is used, how, and importantly, where it's processed and stored (or *not* stored).

---

#### 1. Hand Tracking and Eye Tracking Data (Optional)

*   **What it is:** Data about your hand movements (e.g., gestures, finger positions) and the direction of your eye gaze.
*   **How Immersed Uses It:** To allow you to interact with virtual objects, menus, and navigate the VR environment using your hands or eyes.
*   **Privacy Details:**
    *   **Processing:** Entirely processed **locally on your device** (your VR headset).
    *   **Storage:** **NOT stored** on Immersed's servers.
    *   **Identification:** **NOT used to identify you personally.**
    *   **Purpose:** Solely to facilitate your interaction within the virtual environment.
    *   **Optional:** You enable these features.

    **Visual Concept:**
    👁️‍🗨️✋➡️ 🧠 (Your Headset's Local Processor) ❌☁️ (No Cloud Storage)
    *Imagine your eyes and hands generating data that only stays and gets processed *inside* your headset, never leaving it.*

---

#### 2. Passthrough Camera Data (Optional)

*   **What it is:** Live video feed from the external cameras on your VR headset.
*   **How Immersed Uses It:** To display your real-world surroundings *within* the virtual environment, allowing you to see through the headset.
*   **Privacy Details:**
    *   **Processing:** Entirely processed **locally on your device** (your VR headset).
    *   **Storage:** **NOT stored, collected, or transmitted** by Immersed.
    *   **Purpose:** Only to provide the "passthrough" functionality (seeing your physical room while in VR).
    *   **Optional:** You choose to enable this view.

    **Visual Concept:**
    📹➡️ 🧠 (Your Headset's Local Processor) ➡️ 🖥️ (Your Virtual View) ❌☁️ (No Cloud Storage)
    *Think of the headset's cameras feeding directly to your display, like a live webcam that never records or sends anything out.*

---

#### 3. Microphone Data (Optional)

*   **What it is:** Your voice and other audio captured by your headset's microphone.
*   **How Immersed Uses It:** For voice chat and other audio features, enabling communication with other users.
*   **Privacy Details:**
    *   **Transmission:** Transmitted to Immersed servers **for the purpose of facilitating communication**.
    *   **Storage:** Immersed **does NOT store recordings** of your audio data on their servers after communication ends, *except* for technical processing or legal obligations.
    *   **Usage:** **NOT used for targeted advertising or profiling.**
    *   **Optional:** You enable microphone access.

    **Visual Concept:**
    🎤 ➡️ ☁️ (Immersed Server for Real-time Chat) 🗣️ (Other Users) 🔄 (Temporary Transit) ❌💾 (No Permanent Recording)
    *Imagine your voice traveling through their servers like a phone call – it's live, but generally not recorded for later use by Immersed for profiling.*

---

#### 4. Headset Position and Movement Data (Accelerometer, Gyroscope, etc.)

*   **What it is:** Data from internal motion sensors (like accelerometers and gyroscopes) that track your headset's orientation, position, and movement in space.
*   **How Immersed Uses It:** To enable spatial tracking (knowing where you are in VR) and navigation (moving around the virtual world), and to improve the performance and user experience of their services.
*   **Privacy Details:**
    *   **Collection:** This data **is collected** by Immersed.
    *   **Anonymity:** Generally **anonymous and aggregated**. This means they look at patterns from many users, not individual movements tied back to you.
    *   **Purpose:** Essential for the VR experience itself and for improving the app's tracking accuracy and overall functionality.

    **Visual Concept:**
    ↔️⬆️⬇️ (Headset Movement/Rotation) ➡️ ☁️ (Immersed Server) 📈📊 (Aggregated, Anonymous Data) 🚀 (Product Improvement)
    *Think of your headset constantly telling the app, "I'm here, I'm facing this way, I just moved this much." This essential data helps the virtual world respond correctly, and Immersed uses the overall anonymous patterns to make their system better.*

---

In summary, Immersed emphasizes **local processing** for sensitive data like hand/eye tracking and passthrough camera feeds, ensuring it never leaves your device. For microphone data, it's transmitted for communication but generally not stored. Headset movement data, crucial for VR, is collected but handled in an anonymous and aggregated manner for performance improvements.
