### Immersed Privacy Policy: Understanding Sensor Data

Immersed's services, deeply rooted in Virtual Reality (VR), naturally rely on various sensors to create an immersive and interactive experience. Their policy details what kind of sensor data is collected, how it's processed, and for what purpose.

---

#### 1. What Sensor Data is Collected?

Immersed may collect data from the sensors on your VR headset and other devices you use with their services.

*   **Movement & Positional Data**
    *   **What it is:** Information about how your head and hands move in space.
    *   **Example:** When you turn your head to look around a virtual room or reach out to grab a virtual object.
    *   **Visual:** `🏃‍♂️✨ (Head & Hand Tracking)`

*   **Eye Tracking Data**
    *   **What it is:** Information about where your eyes are looking and how they move.
    *   **Example:** Used for features like gaze interaction or foveated rendering (optimizing graphics where you're looking).
    *   **Visual:** `👁️✨ (Gaze Direction)`

*   **Audio Data**
    *   **What it is:** Sounds picked up by the microphones on your device.
    *   **Example:** For voice commands, spatial audio, or communicating with others in VR.
    *   **Visual:** `🎤✨ (From Microphones)`

*   **Image & Video Data**
    *   **What it is:** Visual information from your device's cameras.
    *   **Example:** Specifically collected for features like hand tracking (seeing your real hands to translate them into VR) and pass-through (seeing your real-world surroundings through the headset cameras).
    *   **Visual:** `📸✨ (From Cameras - Hand Tracking, Passthrough)`

---

#### 2. How is This Sensor Data Handled?

The policy emphasizes specific measures regarding the processing and storage of this sensitive data.

*   **Primary Processing: On-Device**
    *   **How:** Most sensor data (especially eye, hand, and camera-based image/video) is processed directly on your VR headset, not on Immersed's servers.
    *   **Meaning:** This means the raw, detailed sensor information largely stays on your local device.
    *   **Visual:** `⚙️ (On-Device Processing)`

*   **Limited Server Storage (Opt-In/Necessary)**
    *   **When:** This data is *not* stored on Immersed's servers unless it's absolutely necessary for a specific feature, or if you explicitly opt-in.
    *   **Example:** If you choose to send diagnostic data for troubleshooting, some sensor-related information might be included.
    *   **Visual:** `☁️🚫 (Not Stored on Servers unless Opt-In/Necessary)`

*   **Sensitive Data Classification**
    *   **Importance:** Immersed treats eye tracking data and hand tracking data as **sensitive personal information** where required by applicable law.
    *   **Visual:** `🔒 (Sensitive Information)`

---

#### 3. Why is This Sensor Data Collected? (Purpose)

The primary reason for collecting sensor data is to enable and enhance the core VR experience.

*   **Immersive VR Experience:**
    *   **Purpose:** To facilitate natural interactions.
    *   **Examples:** Hand tracking allows you to manipulate virtual objects directly; eye tracking enables gaze-based interactions or foveated rendering (optimizing graphics where you are looking).
    *   **Visual:** `✨ (Enhance VR Experience)`

*   **Service Improvement & Personalization:**
    *   **Purpose:** To make the Immersed service better, more responsive, and tailored to you.
    *   **Examples:** Improving the accuracy of tracking, adapting the virtual environment to your movements, and for general research and development.
    *   **Visual:** `📈 (Improve Services & Personalization)`

---

#### 4. What Sensor-Related Data is NOT Collected?

Immersed explicitly states what they do *not* collect in relation to biometrics for identification purposes.

*   **No Biometric Identifiers for Identification:**
    *   **What it means:** They do not intentionally collect or process biometric identifiers like fingerprints or facial scans that are used for identification purposes through their services.
    *   **Visual:** `🙅‍♀️ (No ID Biometrics)`

---

**In essence:** Immersed collects various forms of sensor data (movement, eye, audio, camera) primarily to power its VR experiences and improve its services. Crucially, most of this sensitive data is processed directly on your device and is only stored on their servers under very specific, often opt-in, circumstances.
