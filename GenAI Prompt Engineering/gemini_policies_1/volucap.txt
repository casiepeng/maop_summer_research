The Volucap Privacy Policy details the capture of "volumetric data" to create immersive 3D video content. This process heavily relies on various sensors to gather comprehensive information about you.

Here's a breakdown of the sensor details, explained with words and descriptive "visualizations":

---

### **1. 📸 Cameras & 📉 Depth Sensors (The Eyes of Volucap)**

Imagine a room filled with advanced cameras and depth sensors, like an array of **digital eyes** scanning a space. These aren't just regular cameras; they work together to capture you in three dimensions.

*   **What they capture:**
    *   **Volumetric Data:** This is the core. Think of it as a "3D video" of you. It's not just a flat image; it captures your **body shape, movements, posture, and gestures** in a fully realized 3D space. Visualize a **wireframe human figure** moving fluidly, or a **digital avatar** accurately mirroring your real-world actions.
    *   **3D Models of Your Body:** From the volumetric data, the system constructs detailed **3D digital replicas** of your physical form. Picture a **sculpted digital twin** of yourself, capturing your unique proportions and contours.
    *   **Image Data for Calibration:** Even before recording, these sensors capture images used to fine-tune the system, ensuring precise measurements and tracking. Imagine a **calibration grid** overlaying the space, ensuring every point is accurately mapped.

---

### **2. 🎙️ Microphones (The Ears of Volucap)**

Alongside the visual capture, Volucap's system also listens.

*   **What they capture:**
    *   **Audio Data:** This includes your **voice** and any **sounds** present in the recording environment. Think of it like a **digital ear** picking up conversations, laughter, or background noises.
    *   **Voice Recordings and Transcripts:** Your spoken words can be recorded and potentially converted into text (transcribed). Visualize **sound waves** being captured and then transformed into **lines of text**.

---

### **3. 🧠 Inferred Data (The "Reading Between the Lines" from Sensors)**

This is a critical aspect. While the sensors capture raw data, the Volucap system *analyzes* this data to infer more nuanced (and sensitive) information about you.

*   **What's Inferred from Sensor Input:**
    *   **Facial Expressions:** From the 3D capture of your face, the system can interpret your emotions. Picture a **smiley face, a frown, or a surprised expression** being digitally recognized from your facial movements.
    *   **Body Language:** Your posture, gestures, and overall movements (captured by the volumetric sensors) are analyzed to understand your non-verbal cues. Imagine a **stick figure demonstrating anger, joy, or thoughtfulness** simply by its pose.
    *   **Emotions:** By combining facial expressions and body language analysis, the system can infer your emotional state. Visualize a **heart icon changing colors** based on interpreted mood.

    **Key Point:** The policy notes that data on your "facial expressions, body language, and emotions" may be processed, and this falls under "special categories of personal data" (like health or biometric data), requiring specific consent.

---

### **Purpose of Sensor Data Collection:**

All this rich sensor data is collected primarily for:

*   **Creating High-Quality 3D Video Content:** The core service.
*   **Developing & Improving Services:** Using your data to make their capture technology better and more accurate.
*   **Personalizing Your Experience:** Tailoring content or interactions based on your captured movements and expressions.

---

In essence, Volucap's sensors act as sophisticated digital eyes and ears, not just recording what you do and say, but also interpreting your physical and emotional state, all to construct and enhance your 3D digital presence.
