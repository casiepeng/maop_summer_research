### ImmerseLearn Privacy Policy: Sensor Data Explained

ImmerseLearn collects specific types of data directly from the devices you use to access their immersive learning services (e.g., VR headsets, smart devices, etc.). This information helps them operate, improve, personalize, and secure your experience.

#### **What Sensor Data is Collected?**

The policy outlines several categories of "Sensory and Biometric Information" that are gathered:

**1. 👁️ Visual Sensory Data (What the System "Sees" or Tracks):**
*   **Eye Gaze:**
    *   **What it is:** Where your eyes are looking within the virtual or augmented environment.
    *   **Visualization:** Imagine a digital "spotlight" constantly tracking your point of focus, like a mouse pointer, but for your eyes.
    *   **Why:** To understand your attention, interaction with objects, and potentially to personalize content or adapt the experience (e.g., auto-focusing on what you're looking at).
*   **Hand Movements:**
    *   **What it is:** The position, orientation, and motion of your hands.
    *   **Visualization:** Think of a virtual "glove" mirroring your precise hand gestures and actions (e.g., pointing, grasping, typing in VR).
    *   **Why:** For interaction with virtual objects, recognizing commands, and providing realistic physical presence.
*   **Body Movements:**
    *   **What it is:** Your overall posture, position, and motion within the tracked space.
    *   **Visualization:** Like a digital "puppet" replicating your physical actions (walking, turning, ducking, standing).
    *   **Why:** To navigate immersive environments, engage in physical activities, and ensure your virtual self matches your real movements.

**2. 👂 Audio Sensory Data (What the System "Hears"):**
*   **Voice:**
    *   **What it is:** Your speech patterns, tone, and general audio input.
    *   **Visualization:** A microphone icon constantly listening and capturing your spoken words.
    *   **Why:** For communication within the platform, potential voice analysis, and enabling voice commands.
*   **Voice Commands:**
    *   **What it is:** Specific spoken words or phrases recognized by the system to trigger actions.
    *   **Visualization:** A speech bubble appearing with the recognized command (e.g., "Open menu," "Select item"), immediately executing an action.
    *   **Why:** For hands-free control and navigation within the immersive environment.

**3. ❤️ Other Physiological / Biometric Inputs (Body Signals):**
*   **Heart Rate:**
    *   **What it is:** Your pulse, typically collected via compatible wearable devices (e.g., smartwatches, fitness trackers synced with the platform).
    *   **Visualization:** A pulsing heart icon or an ECG line showing your beats per minute.
    *   **Why:** Potentially to gauge stress levels, engagement, or tailor adaptive learning experiences based on physiological responses.
*   **Skin Temperature:**
    *   **What it is:** Your body's surface temperature, also likely from compatible wearable devices.
    *   **Visualization:** A thermometer icon indicating whether you're cool, warm, or hot.
    *   **Why:** Similar to heart rate, could be used to infer states like comfort, exertion, or for health-related features within the learning environment.
