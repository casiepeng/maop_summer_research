### VRChat Privacy Policy: Your Sensors in the Metaverse

VRChat collects information about how you interact with the virtual world, and a significant part of that comes from the sensors in your VR (Virtual Reality) devices. Think of these sensors as the "eyes" and "ears" (and "body trackers") of your VR headset and controllers, allowing VRChat to understand your presence and actions in the digital space.

---

#### 1. Core VR Device & Movement Data (Always Collected for Core Functionality)

**What it is:** This is the foundational data collected from your VR headset and controllers. It tells VRChat where you are, which way you're looking, and how you're moving your head and hands.

*   **Your Position:**
    *   **Visualization:** Imagine a tiny *`X, Y, Z coordinate`* system in the virtual world, where VRChat knows exactly where your head (and thus your avatar) is located at all times.
    *   **Explanation:** Where you are in the 3D virtual space (e.g., standing in front of someone, walking across a room).

*   **Your Orientation:**
    *   **Visualization:** Picture an *`arrow pointing out from your avatar's head`* or controllers, showing the direction you're facing or pointing.
    *   **Explanation:** Which way your head is turned, or which way your controllers are aimed (e.g., looking at a friend, pointing at an object).

*   **Your Movement:**
    *   **Visualization:** Think of a *`trail of footprints`* or a *`motion blur`* behind your avatar as you walk, run, or jump. This also includes the movements of your hands and arms via controllers.
    *   **Explanation:** How you're moving through the world (e.g., walking forward, turning around, gesturing with your hands).

**Why it's collected:**
*   **To render your avatar:** This data is essential for your avatar to move realistically in the virtual world, matching your physical movements.
    *   *Visualization:* Your digital self (`🧍`) perfectly mirroring your real self's (`👤`) actions.
*   **To facilitate social interactions:** Allows other users to see your movements and understand your non-verbal cues.
    *   *Visualization:* Seeing a friend (`➡️`) turn to look at you, or raise their hand (`👋`) to wave.
*   **To improve the overall user experience:** Helps VRChat ensure smooth and responsive interactions.

---

#### 2. Advanced Expressive Data (Collected Only if Your Device Supports & You Opt-In)

This is more sensitive data that newer VR headsets with advanced sensors can collect. VRChat emphasizes that this data is **optional** and **only collected if your device supports it AND you choose to enable it.**

*   **Your Gaze & Eye Movements:**
    *   **Visualization:** Imagine a *`laser pointer coming out of your avatar's eyes`*, showing exactly where you're looking, and tiny *`arcs representing the movement of your pupils`*.
    *   **Explanation:** Where your eyes are focused within the virtual environment and how they move (e.g., scanning a room, looking directly at someone's avatar).

*   **Your Facial Expressions:**
    *   **Visualization:** Picture a *`live facial recognition grid`* or *`tiny cameras mapping the muscles around your mouth and eyes`*, allowing your avatar's face to mimic your real-world expressions.
    *   **Explanation:** How your face is moving (e.g., smiling, frowning, blinking, talking, raising an eyebrow).

**Why it's collected (if enabled):**
*   **To enhance avatar realism:** Makes your avatar's face and eyes appear more lifelike and expressive.
    *   *Visualization:* An avatar's face (`😊`) genuinely reflecting your happiness.
*   **To enable more expressive communication:** Allows for richer non-verbal interactions that convey emotion more effectively.
    *   *Visualization:* A subtle nod (`点头`) or an eye-roll (`🙄`) from your avatar that immediately communicates your feelings.

---

#### Your Control Over Sensor Data

VRChat states you have choices regarding the collection of this data:

*   **Device Settings:** You may be able to adjust privacy settings directly on your VR device (e.g., Meta Quest, Valve Index) to limit what data it sends.
    *   *Visualization:* A *`gear icon`* on your VR headset's dashboard leading to `[Privacy Settings]`.
*   **VRChat Settings:** Within the VRChat application itself, there may be settings to further control data collection, especially for the more advanced gaze and facial tracking.
    *   *Visualization:* A *`toggle switch`* within VRChat's in-game menu: `[👁️ Eye Tracking ON/OFF]` `[😀 Face Tracking ON/OFF]`.

---

**In essence, VRChat uses the sensors in your VR equipment to bring your physical presence and expressions into the virtual world, making interactions feel more natural. While basic movement is fundamental to the experience, advanced eye and face tracking are optional and under your control, offering a deeper level of avatar realism for those who choose it.**
