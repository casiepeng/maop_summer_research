The privacy policy for the "3D Cinema" service outlines specific details regarding the collection and use of sensor data from your device.

Here's a summary of the key information, with visualizations to make it more understandable:

---

### 🕵️‍♀️ **What Sensor Data is Collected?**

The policy explicitly states that as part of "Device Information," they collect **"sensor data (such as accelerometer, gyroscope, and compass data)."**

*   **1. Accelerometer:**
    *   `▵`
    *   **What it does:** Measures motion, tilt, and shaking of your device.
    *   **In 3D Cinema:** Helps understand if you're moving your device, tilting it, or if it's being jostled.

*   **2. Gyroscope:**
    *   `🔄`
    *   **What it does:** Tracks the orientation and rotational movement of your device, understanding how it's turning in space.
    *   **In 3D Cinema:** Crucial for head-tracking in VR/3D environments, allowing the virtual scene to react to your head movements.

*   **3. Compass:**
    *   `🧭`
    *   **What it does:** Detects your device's heading relative to magnetic north.
    *   **In 3D Cinema:** Provides directional awareness, ensuring the virtual environment aligns with your real-world orientation.

*   **Additional (Consent-Based) Location Data:**
    *   `📍`
    *   While not explicitly listed as a "sensor," the policy notes that "with your consent, we may collect information about your precise or approximate location," which typically relies on GPS and other location-based sensors.

### 🎯 **Why is Sensor Data Collected? (How it's Used)**

The policy states that information (including sensor data) is used for several key purposes:

*   **1. To Provide & Maintain Services:**
    *   `🎬`
    *   **Purpose:** To deliver the core functionalities of the 3D Cinema.
    *   **Sensor Role:** Essential for creating immersive 3D/VR experiences, enabling features like looking around by moving your device, or stabilizing the virtual view.

*   **2. To Improve & Develop New Services:**
    *   `📈`
    *   **Purpose:** To understand user behavior and preferences, and to make the app better.
    *   **Sensor Role:** Analyzing how users interact with the 3D environment (e.g., how they move their heads, what they look at) to refine features and develop new ones.

*   **3. To Personalize Your Experience:**
    *   `👤✨`
    *   **Purpose:** To offer customized content and features.
    *   **Sensor Role:** Tailoring the viewing experience or content recommendations based on how you naturally interact with the app.
