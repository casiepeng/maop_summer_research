Based on the privacy policy provided, here are the details regarding sensors:

For certain features, particularly those involving augmented reality (AR) or virtual reality (VR) experiences, Voluverse may access and process data from your device’s sensors.

This sensor data includes:
*   **Accelerometer, gyroscope, and magnetometer data** for motion tracking.
*   **Camera data** for spatial mapping and object recognition.

The primary purpose for using this sensor data is to enable and enhance your immersive experiences.
