The Voluverse privacy policy indicates the collection of several types of data directly or indirectly derived from sensors, primarily for tracking user interactions and environment:

*   **Tracking data:** Information related to physical movements and interactions within the Voluverse, including head movements, hand movements, and controller inputs.
*   **Spatial data:** Information related to the physical environment, such as the location of objects and surfaces within your VR space.
*   **Biometric data:** If users choose to enable certain features, this may include eye-tracking or facial expression data to enhance the experience.
*   **Voice data:** If users choose to use the voice chat function, voice data may be collected to enable communication with other users.
