The privacy policy details the collection of two main types of sensor-related information:

1.  **Location Information:**
    *   Approximate location (country and city) is collected from your device, deduced from your IP address.
    *   When you use the services, more precise location information may be collected using technologies such as GPS, Wi-Fi, or cellular data.
    *   This data is used to provide you with location-based services and personalize your experience.

2.  **Device Sensor Data:**
    *   Specific device sensor data, such as from accelerometers, gyroscopes, and compasses, may be collected *if you grant permission*.
    *   This data is collected to help understand your device's orientation and movement.
