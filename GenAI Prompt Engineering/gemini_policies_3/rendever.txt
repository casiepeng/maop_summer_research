Based on the privacy policy provided for Rendever's Alcove platform, here are the details regarding the collection of data derived from sensors:

The policy states that when you use their services, they collect:

*   **Voice, hand, and gaze data:** This includes information such as your voice commands, gestures, and eye movements.
*   **Other biometric data:** This data is generated from your use of the service.
*   **GPS location:** This information is collected as part of the technical information from your devices used to access the services.
