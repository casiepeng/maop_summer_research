Based on the Immersed.com privacy policy, here are the details regarding sensor data collection:

When using Immersed VR software and hardware, the company may collect:

*   **Tracking data:** This includes data related to your head and hand movements, eye gaze, and body posture within the VR environment. This data is used to provide and improve the VR experience, such as enabling interactions with virtual objects and tracking your progress.
*   **Audio data:** With your permission, audio data may be collected through your device's microphone for features like voice commands and communication within the VR environment.
*   **Biometric data:** If you enable certain features, eye-tracking data may be collected. This data is used for purposes like foveated rendering (optimizing visual quality) or user authentication. This biometric data is processed on-device whenever possible and is not shared with third parties unless you explicitly consent for a specific purpose (e.g., sharing a facial avatar with other users).
