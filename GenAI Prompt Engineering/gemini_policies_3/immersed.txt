Immersed collects various sensor data directly from your VR/AR/XR headset and controllers. This includes:

*   **Head and Controller Position and Orientation Data:** Used to track your movements within the virtual environment.
*   **Eye-tracking Data:** If enabled on your device.
*   **Hand-tracking Data:** If enabled on your device.
*   **Microphone Data:** Utilized for features such as voice commands, spatial audio, and communication.
*   **Camera Data:** Used for features like passthrough, spatial anchors, and environmental understanding.
*   **Other Sensor Data:** This can include data from ambient light sensors, proximity sensors, and haptic feedback data.

Most of this data is processed locally on your device to enable core functionalities like tracking your movements. However, some of this data may be transmitted to Immersed's servers for specific features (e.g., voice chat, cloud-based spatial anchors) or for analytics purposes.
