Based on Google's Privacy Policy, here are the details regarding sensors:

Google collects information from your devices' sensors. This includes:

*   **Location determination:** GPS and "other sensor data" are used to determine your location with varying degrees of accuracy.
*   **Device information:** They also collect general information about your devices' settings and sensors.

This sensor-derived location information is used to help provide features such as driving directions, search results for nearby places, and location-based advertisements.
