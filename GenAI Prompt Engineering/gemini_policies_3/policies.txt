Based on Google's Privacy Policy, here's a summary of details regarding sensors:

Google collects information from and about the devices you use to access their services, and this includes data from **sensors**.

Specifically, the policy mentions:
*   **Accelerometers** (to detect movement and screen orientation).
*   **Gyroscopes** (to detect rotation and orientation).
*   **Magnetometers** (to detect compass direction).
*   **GPS and other sensors** (used to determine precise location).

This sensor data is used to understand how you interact with your device and their services, provide features like screen rotation or location-based services, and to generally improve their offerings.
