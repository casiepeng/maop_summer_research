VRChat collects information directly from your VR headset and any VR accessories you choose to use, such as eye trackers, face trackers, full-body trackers, and haptic devices.

This sensor-derived data includes details about:
*   Your avatar's movement and position.
*   Your facial expressions and eye gaze.
*   Your hand and finger movements.

This information, which may be considered biometric data in some jurisdictions, is used to animate your avatar for you and others in VRChat, and for VRChat's internal analytics to improve the user experience.

Additionally, VRChat collects voice data from your microphone when you use it within the platform. This serves to enable others to hear you and is also used for internal analytics to enhance the service.

VRChat also automatically collects telemetry regarding your virtual world position, locomotion, and interactions with the platform and other users. This is used to understand usage patterns, improve functionality, and detect/prevent fraud or abuse.
