The privacy policy states that it automatically collects several types of sensor-related information:

*   **Motion Information:** This includes data on your head, hand, and controller movements.
*   **Eye-Tracking Data:** Details like your eye position and movements are collected.
*   **Physical Characteristics:** This covers precise physical characteristics such as your estimated hand size and other biometric data like your estimated interpupillary distance (IPD).
*   **Physical Environment:** Information about your physical surroundings, including the dimensions and contours of your play space.
*   **Approximate Location Data:** This includes device location and time zone.

This collected sensor-related data, particularly information about your physical environment, is used to create virtual boundaries or safe zones within the virtual environment.
