Based on the Immerse Learn privacy policy:

The policy states that Immerse Learn automatically collects "sensor data" from the device you use to access their Services. This data specifically includes, but is not limited to:

*   Your headset’s position and orientation
*   Gaze direction
*   Eye tracking
*   Hand movements
*   Other physical inputs you make while using their immersive learning experiences.

This sensor data is collected to provide and maintain their services, improve and personalize your experience, develop new features and functionalities, and for research and development purposes, including analyzing user behavior and preferences. While they may share information with service providers or for legal reasons, aggregated or anonymized data, which could include sensor data, may be used for research and analytics.
