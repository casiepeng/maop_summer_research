Based on the Immerselearn privacy policy, while the term "sensors" is not explicitly used, the policy details the collection of data that originates from device sensors, particularly from VR/AR headsets or other similar devices.

Specifically, the policy states it collects:

*   **Physical movements:** Data related to your overall physical movements within the virtual environment.
*   **Head movements:** Information about how you move and orient your head.
*   **Gaze direction:** Data indicating where your eyes are looking.
*   **Hand movements:** Information regarding the position and actions of your hands.

This data is described as essential for providing the immersive experience and for improving their services. Additionally, approximate location information, derived from your IP address, is also collected.
