The Immerse Learn privacy policy states that for immersive experiences (e.g., virtual reality or augmented reality), they may collect data generated by the sensors on your device.

Specifically, this includes:
*   Data related to your **head and hand movements, gaze direction, body posture, and other physical interactions within the virtual environment.** This data is essential for providing the immersive experience and improving functionality.
*   **Device-level sensor data (e.g., accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer)** to enable features like tracking, spatial mapping, and environmental understanding.
*   They **do not intentionally collect biometric data** such as fingerprints or facial scans. If biometric data is collected, it's explicitly requested and consented to for specific features (e.g., avatar creation that uses facial scanning), and in such cases, the data is processed locally on your device and not stored by Immerse Learn.
*   If location services are enabled on your device, they may collect **general location information (e.g., IP-based location)**.
