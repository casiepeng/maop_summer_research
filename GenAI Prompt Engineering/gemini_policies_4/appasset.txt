**1. Information We Collect**

We collect information about you in various ways when you use our Services.

**1.1 Information You Provide to Us**
We collect information you provide directly to us. For example, we collect information when you:
*   **Create an account or profile:** We may collect your username, password, email address, phone number, date of birth, and any other information you provide as part of your profile.
*   **Participate in interactive features:** We collect information when you participate in polls, contests, promotions, surveys, or other interactive features of our Services.
*   **Communicate with us:** We collect information when you communicate with us via email, chat, or other channels, such as customer support inquiries.
*   **Make purchases:** If you make purchases through our Services, we may collect payment information (such as credit card details), billing address, and shipping information. We typically use third-party payment processors who handle your payment information directly.
*   **Upload content:** If our Services allow you to upload or create content (e.g., photos, videos, audio, text), we collect that content and associated metadata.

**1.2 Information We Collect Automatically**
When you access or use our Services, we automatically collect certain information about your device and your use of our Services. This information includes:
*   **Log Information:** We collect log files, which include your IP address, browser type, operating system, pages viewed, access times, and the page you visited before navigating to our Services.
*   **Device Information:** We collect information about the device you use to access our Services, including hardware model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers (e.g., IMEI, MAC address), mobile network information, and crash data.
*   **Usage Information:** We collect information about your activity on our Services, such as the features you use, the games you play, the content you view, and the time spent on our Services.
*   **Location Information:** With your consent, we may collect precise geolocation information from your device. You can disable location tracking at any time through your device settings. We may also infer your approximate location based on your IP address.
*   **Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies:** We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information and provide functionality.
    *   **Cookies** are small data files stored on your device that help us improve our Services and your experience, see which areas and features of our Services are popular, and count visits.
    *   **Web beacons** (also known as "pixel tags" or "clear GIFs") are electronic images that may be used in our Services or emails and help deliver cookies, count visits, understand usage and campaign effectiveness, and determine whether an email has been opened and acted upon.
*   **Third-Party Analytics:** We may use third-party analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Unity Analytics) to collect and analyze usage information, generate reports, and assist with fraud prevention. These services may use cookies and other tracking technologies to collect information about your use of our Services.

**1.3 Information We Collect from Other Sources**
We may obtain information from other sources and combine it with information we collect through our Services. For example, if you:
*   **Link your social media accounts:** If you link your XVERSE account with a third-party social media service (e.g., Facebook, Google), we may receive information such as your name, profile picture, user ID, email address, and friend lists, depending on the privacy settings you have with that service.
