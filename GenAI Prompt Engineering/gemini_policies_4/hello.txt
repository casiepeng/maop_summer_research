**1. COLLECTION OF INFORMATION**

**Information You Provide to Us**

We collect information you provide directly to us. For example, you provide information to us when you:

*   Create an account, provide us with your username, email address, password, birthdate, and in some cases, a third-party application ID (such as a Steam ID or Oculus ID) for authentication purposes.
*   Log in to your account.
*   Manage your profile.
*   Participate in community forums, events, or promotions.
*   Contact us with questions, feedback, or to request technical support.
*   Purchase products or services from us.

**Automatically Collected Information**

We automatically collect certain information about your use of the Services, including:

*   **Log and Usage Information:** We collect log and usage information related to your access to and use of the Services, including the type of device you use, the features you use, the servers you connect to, access times, IP address, and browser type.
*   **Device Information:** We collect information about the computer or mobile device you use to access our Services, including the hardware model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
*   **Hardware and Software Information:** We collect information regarding the hardware and software you use while accessing the Services, including information about the virtual reality headset you are using, GPU type, and other diagnostic information regarding the performance of the App.
*   **Information Collected by Cookies and Other Tracking Technologies:** We use various technologies to collect information, including cookies and web beacons. Cookies are small data files stored on your hard drive or in device memory that help us improve our Services and your experience, see which areas and features of our Services are popular, and count visits. Web beacons (also known as “pixel tags” or “clear GIFs”) are electronic images that may be used in our Services or emails and help deliver cookies, count visits, and understand usage and campaign effectiveness. For more information about how we use cookies and your choices, please see our [Cookie Policy](https://hello.vrchat.com/cookie-policy).
*   **Information from Content:** We collect information about the content that you create and upload to the Services, including worlds, avatars, and other digital assets (e.g., file size, upload time, etc.). We do not collect or store the actual audio or visual content of your communications within VRChat (such as voice chat or webcam streams). For more information, please see “Information We Don’t Collect” below.

**Information from Third Parties**

We obtain information from third-party sources. For example:

*   If you access the Services through a third-party platform (such as Steam or Meta/Oculus), we may collect certain account information from that platform, such as your username or ID.
*   We may collect information from third-party services that you link to the Services (such as YouTube or Twitch).
*   We obtain information from analytics providers and advertisers.

**Information We Don’t Collect**

We do not collect or store:

*   The actual audio or visual content of your communications within VRChat (such as voice chat or webcam streams).
*   Your physical location.
*   Your real name (unless you choose to provide it to us directly).
*   Information that can be used to identify your real-world identity, unless you choose to provide it to us directly.

This includes exchanging information with other companies and organizations for fraud protection and spam/malware prevention.

*   **With your consent:** We may disclose information about you to third parties with your consent or at your direction.
*   **Aggregated or de-identified information:** We may share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.

**8. ADDITIONAL DISCLOSURES FOR INDIVIDUALS IN EUROPE**

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, you have certain rights and protections under applicable law regarding the processing of your personal data.

We process personal data for the purposes described in the “Use of Information” section above. Our legal bases for processing personal data include:

*   **Necessity for the performance of a contract:** We process your personal data when it is necessary for the performance of a contract with you or to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract. This includes processing for account creation, providing the Services, and customer support.
*   **Necessity for compliance with a legal obligation:** We may process your personal data when it is necessary to comply with a legal obligation to which we are subject, such as for tax and accounting purposes.
*   **Necessity for the purposes of our legitimate interests:** We may process your personal data when it is necessary for the purposes of our legitimate interests, provided that these interests are not overridden by your fundamental rights and freedoms. Our legitimate interests include improving our Services, preventing fraud, and marketing.

**Data Retention**

We store personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which we collect it, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. We will also retain your personal data as necessary to comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

**Data Subject Requests**

If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and the right to data portability. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@vrchat.com.

As noted in Section 5, VRChat is headquartered in the United States, and we have operations and service providers in the United States and other countries. Therefore, your personal data may be transferred to, or stored or accessed in, jurisdictions outside of the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland that may not provide equivalent levels of data protection as your home jurisdiction. We will take steps to ensure that your personal data receives an adequate level of protection in the jurisdictions in which we process it. Where required by law, we will implement appropriate safeguards for international data transfers, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or other approved transfer mechanisms.
