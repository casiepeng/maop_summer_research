Based on the privacy policy for Voluverse, here's a summary of details regarding sensors:

The Voluverse app utilizes sensors, primarily in connection with its "Body Scan" feature and for interactive elements within the experience.

1.  **Body Scan:** This feature specifically uses **depth sensors (such as Lidar) and cameras** to capture and process data. This sensor data is used to create 3D models of your body, including your body dimensions, shape, and facial features. The data derived from the Body Scan, particularly facial features, is considered biometric data.

2.  **Other Sensory Inputs:** The policy also indicates the collection of data from other user interactions based on sensory input, including:
    *   **Voice data** (implying the use of a microphone).
    *   **Eye movements** (implying eye-tracking technology).
    *   **Body motion and hand gestures** (implying motion tracking technology).
