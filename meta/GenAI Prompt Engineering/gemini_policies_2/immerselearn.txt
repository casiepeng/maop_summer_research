I've reviewed the ImmerseLearn privacy policy.

Regarding sensors, the policy states that they automatically collect certain information from your devices, particularly those related to Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), and Extended Reality (XR) experiences.

While it doesn't list specific *types* of sensors (like eye-tracking, heart rate, or gaze), it does indicate they collect:

1.  **Hardware details:** This includes information about your specific VR/AR/XR headset type and controllers. These devices inherently contain various sensors (e.g., for motion, position, haptics).
2.  **Tracking data:** This is the most relevant point for sensors. It refers to the data generated by the sensors within your headset and controllers, which captures your movement, position, and interactions within the immersive environment (e.g., where your head is oriented, how your hands are moving, your position in space).

In essence, ImmerseLearn collects data produced by the sensors in your VR/AR/XR equipment to understand how you are using their application and interacting with the virtual world. They do not explicitly mention collecting biometric data like heart rate or detailed eye-tracking data, but the "tracking data" could encompass general head and hand movement tracking.
