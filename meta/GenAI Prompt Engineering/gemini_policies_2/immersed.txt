Based on the Immersed privacy policy, here's a summary of the details regarding sensors:

Immersed collects specific sensor data related to your interactions within their virtual environment:

1.  **Physical Movements:** They gather data about your **physical movements**, explicitly mentioning **head and hand movements**.
2.  **Gaze/Eye Movement:** They also collect information about your **gaze** or **eye movement**, specifically the **direction of your gaze**.

This sensor data is used for several key purposes:

*   **Service Provision:** To provide and enable their core services, allowing you to interact naturally within the virtual space.
*   **Feature Enablement:** For eye-tracking, specifically to enable features like **foveated rendering** (optimizing visual quality where you're looking) and **virtual object interaction**.
*   **User Experience Improvement:** To understand how users interact with the platform and to continuously enhance the overall user experience.

In essence, Immersed uses data from your head, hand, and eye movements to make the VR experience functional, interactive, and to constantly improve it.
