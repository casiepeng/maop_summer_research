### **Alcove's Privacy Policy: Sensor Data Explained**

Alcove, as a VR/AR platform, needs to interact with your movements and environment to provide immersive experiences. Here's what they say about the sensor data they collect:

---

#### 1. **Motion & Gaze Data** 🏃‍♂️👀
*   **What it is:** If you're using a VR/AR headset, Alcove collects data about:
    *   **Your Head Movements:** 🧑‍🦰➡️⬅️⬆️⬇️ (How you turn or tilt your head).
    *   **Your Hand Movements:** ✋↔️🖐️ (How you move your hands, which often corresponds to controllers).
    *   **Your Gaze Direction:** 👀🎯 (Where your eyes are looking within the VR/AR environment).
*   **Why they collect it:**
    *   To make the experience feel **immersive and real** 🌌.
    *   To allow you to **interact naturally** within the virtual world 🎮.
    *   To **analyze how users engage** with the application and improve it 📊.
*   **Storage:** This data is typically processed and stored on their servers.

---

#### 2. **Voice Data** 🗣️🎤
*   **What it is:** Recordings of your voice commands if you use voice-activated features.
*   **Why they collect it:**
    *   To enable **voice commands** (e.g., "Open this," "Go here") 🗣️.
    *   To **improve the accuracy** of their speech recognition 🧠.
*   **Consent:** They only collect this **with your explicit consent** ✅.
*   **Processing:** They aim to process this data **locally on your device** where possible. If it needs to be sent to their servers for processing, it's typically **anonymized** first 🔐.

---

#### 3. **Other Sensory Data (Environmental/Spatial Awareness)** 🏠🗺️
*   **What it is:** Data from your VR/AR device that maps out your physical surroundings. This is often called "spatial awareness" or "environmental mapping data."
*   **Why they collect it:**
    *   Primarily for **"room-scale experiences,"** so the application knows the boundaries of your play space and you don't bump into real-world objects while in VR ⚠️.
*   **Processing & Storage:** This data is **mostly processed on your device**. It's only stored on their servers if it's **necessary for specific features** and typically **with your explicit consent** if it leaves your device 🤝.

---

#### **What they specifically *DO NOT* collect (regarding location sensors):** 📍🚫
*   Alcove **does not collect precise location information** directly from your device (like GPS coordinates). They may infer an **approximate location** (like your country or city) from your IP address, but this isn't from a device sensor.

---

### **In Simple Terms:**

Imagine your VR/AR headset has "eyes" and "ears" and can sense your basic movements:

*   **Your Movements (Head & Hands) & What You Look At:** They use this to make the virtual world respond to you, just like real life.
*   **Your Voice:** They listen to your commands (if you allow it) to control things and make their voice recognition better.
*   **Your Room's Shape:** Your headset might scan your room so you can move around safely in VR without hitting your furniture.
*   **Your Exact Location:** They are clear they *don't* track your precise location.

This sensor data is primarily used to make your VR/AR experience functional, immersive, and safe.
