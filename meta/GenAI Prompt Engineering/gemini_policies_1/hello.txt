## VRChat Privacy Policy: Your Sensor Data Explained

VRChat explicitly states that when you use VR devices or other peripherals that include sensor capabilities, they may collect "sensor data" from these devices.

---

### 🔍 **What Sensor Data Might Be Collected?**

VRChat's policy highlights several specific types of sensor data:

*   👁️ **Eye-Tracking:** Data about where your eyes are looking.
*   😊 **Face-Tracking:** Information about your facial expressions and movements.
*   🤸 **Full Body Tracking:** Data regarding your body's movements and posture in the virtual space.
*   ✋ **Hand-Tracking:** Details about your hand and finger gestures.
*   🎤 **Microphone Input:** Your voice and any sounds captured by your microphone.

---

### ✨ **Why is This Data Collected?**

The primary purposes for collecting sensor data are to:

*   **Enable & Improve Functionality:** To make the VRChat service work correctly and enhance its features.
*   **🤖 Animate Your Avatar:** To bring your virtual character to life with realistic movements, expressions, and gestures based on your real-world actions.
*   **🤝 Improve Social Interactions:** To make communication and presence feel more natural and immersive with other users in VRChat.
*   **🎮 Enhance the Immersive Experience:** To deepen your sense of presence and engagement within the virtual world.

---

### 🧬 **Biometric Data (With Consent!)**

VRChat mentions that "biometric data" *may also be collected* if you use features that provide it. Crucially, this is only done **with your explicit consent where required by law.**

---

### ⚙️ **Your Control Over Sensor Data**

You're not without options! VRChat states that you can manage the collection of certain sensor data:

*   **Through your device settings:** (e.g., your VR headset's own privacy controls).
*   **Within the VRChat application settings:** Look for options inside the VRChat app itself.

---

### ⚠️ **CRUCIAL PRIVACY ALERT: What Others Can See & Record!**

This is perhaps the most important detail regarding sensor data:

*   **👀 Other users can observe your avatar's movements and actions.**
*   **⏺️ Other users may also be able to *record* your avatar's movements and actions.**

This includes any movements or expressions that are directly derived from your sensor data (e.g., your eye movements, facial expressions, or full body movements).

---

### **In Simple Terms:**

VRChat uses advanced sensor data from your VR equipment (like eye, face, body, and hand tracking, plus your mic) to make your avatar incredibly realistic and your interactions more immersive. While you have some control over what data is shared, remember that **anything your sensors track about you that translates to your avatar's actions in VRChat can be seen and potentially recorded by other users.** So, what you do in real life with these sensors can be "seen" by others in the virtual world.
