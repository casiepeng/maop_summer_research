### **Aura Vision Privacy Policy: Sensor Data at a Glance**

Aura Vision's service collects different types of information, some of which directly involves your device's sensors or data derived from them. The policy is quite clear about *what* is collected and, importantly, *when* your consent is required.

---

#### **1. Direct Sensor Access (Requires Your Explicit Consent!)**

These are the most impactful sensor data points, as they involve direct access to your device's hardware capabilities. Aura Vision emphasizes they *will ask you* before accessing these.

*   **Camera 📸**
    *   **What:** Access to your device's camera.
    *   **Why:** For features like **Augmented Reality (AR) experiences** (overlaying digital content onto your real-world view) or potentially **video calls**.
    *   **Your Control:**
        *   **Visual:** `[App asks for permission] --> [You tap "Allow" or "Deny"]`
        *   **Words:** They will *only* access your camera with your **explicit consent**. You can grant or revoke this access at any time through your device's privacy settings.

*   **Microphone 🎤**
    *   **What:** Access to your device's microphone.
    *   **Why:** For features like **voice commands** or **video calls**.
    *   **Your Control:**
        *   **Visual:** `[App asks for permission] --> [You tap "Allow" or "Deny"]`
        *   **Words:** Similar to the camera, they will *only* access your microphone with your **explicit consent**. You can manage this permission via your device's privacy settings.

*   **Biometric Information (Face/Hand Tracking) 🖐️👁️**
    *   **What:** Data derived from your unique physical characteristics, like facial recognition or hand tracking.
    *   **Why:** If the service incorporates features that use this, such as **facial recognition for authentication** or **hand tracking for AR interactions**.
    *   **Key Detail & Your Control:**
        *   **Visual:** `[Your Face/Hand] --(Scan)--> [Your Device 📱] --(Process On-Device if possible!)--> [App Feature] <--(Maybe to Server with *Separate* Consent for *Core Functionality*)`
        *   **Words:** This is crucial:
            *   They will obtain your **explicit consent** before processing such data.
            *   Whenever possible, this information is **processed on-device** (meaning it stays on your phone, not sent to their servers).
            *   It is **NOT stored on their servers** unless it's strictly necessary for the service's core functionality *and* only with your **separate, explicit consent**. This indicates a high level of control and limited server-side storage for biometrics.

---

#### **2. Location Information (Varies by Precision & Consent)**

Your device has ways to determine its location, and Aura Vision collects this information at different levels of precision.

*   **Approximate Location 📍 (Inferred)**
    *   **What:** A general idea of your location (e.g., city or region).
    *   **How:** This is often inferred from your **IP address** (the unique number identifying your internet connection). It doesn't use your device's GPS or precise sensors directly.
    *   **Visual:** `[Your Device IP Address] --> [Internet Lookup] --> [General Area Map]`
    *   **Words:** Collected automatically, but it's not highly precise.

*   **Precise Location 🌍 (Requires Your Consent!)**
    *   **What:** Your exact geographical coordinates.
    *   **How:** This uses your device's built-in **GPS** or other location sensors (Wi-Fi, cell tower triangulation).
    *   **Your Control:**
        *   **Visual:** `[App asks for permission] --> [You tap "Allow" or "Deny"] --> [GPS Satellite 🛰️]`
        *   **Words:** They will *only* collect your precise location with your **explicit consent**. You can enable or disable location services for the app through your device settings at any time.

---

#### **3. Device & Usage Information (Automatically Collected - Indirect Sensor Link)**

While not direct sensor data, these categories provide information about your device and how you use it, which are foundational to how sensor data might be utilized or correlated.

*   **Device Information 📱**
    *   **What:** Details about the device you're using.
    *   **Examples:** IP address, browser type, operating system, unique device identifiers (a specific ID for your phone/tablet), and mobile network information.
    *   **Visual:** `[Your Phone Hardware & Software]`
    *   **Words:** Collected automatically to help them understand what kind of devices are using their service, crucial for ensuring compatibility with AR features that rely on device capabilities.

*   **Usage Information 📊**
    *   **What:** Data about how you interact with the service.
    *   **Examples:** Which features you use, how often, how you navigate.
    *   **Visual:** `[Your Interaction with App] --(Data Stream)--> [Analytics Dashboard]`
    *   **Words:** This helps them understand user behavior and improve the service. If your "interaction" involves moving your phone for AR, this implicitly ties back to how you use sensor-driven features.

---

#### **Summary of Your Control Over Sensor Data:**

Aura Vision places a strong emphasis on user consent, especially for sensitive sensor data.

*   **Explicit Consent:** For Camera, Microphone, Precise Location, and Biometric Data, they will always ask for your permission first.
*   **Device Settings:** You maintain control over these permissions through your device's privacy settings, allowing you to grant or revoke access at any time.

This policy reflects a commitment to privacy around sensor data, particularly with the on-device processing preference for biometric information.
